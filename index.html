A creative force across every dimension of film — Prem Kumar Thanneru is an actor, director, screenwriter, and editor with over 5 years in the industry. His work is defined by psychological depth, precise visual instinct, and an unwavering commitment to the story.

From developing characters from scratch to orchestrating complete productions, Prem brings a rare dual intelligence: the vulnerability of a performer and the structural rigor of a director. His films have explored themes of grace, morality, and human contradiction — always through a cinematic lens.

Trained in AI & Data Science alongside his filmmaking career, he brings a uniquely analytical creative mind to every project he touches.
